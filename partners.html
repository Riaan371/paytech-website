Our story

Why Paytech exists

Paytech was built on a simple frustration: payroll shouldn't be this hard. Too many business owners lose sleep over PAYE deadlines, missed UIF submissions, and payslips gone wrong — not because they're bad at business, but because payroll was never meant to be a side project.

So we set out to be the partner that takes the weight off, not just another system to manage. Whether you're a five-person team or a multi-site operation, our job stays the same: your people get paid accurately, your business stays compliant, and payroll stops being something you have to think about.

Why choose Paytech

Platform expertise you can rely on

Being certified isn't just a badge — it means our team has completed rigorous training on each platform, stays up to date with every update, and knows how to configure the system to suit your specific business.

Whether you're migrating from one platform to another, starting fresh, or need ongoing support — Paytech has you covered across both platforms.
